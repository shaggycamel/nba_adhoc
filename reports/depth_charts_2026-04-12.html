What to trust

  • Expected minutes (p_play × minutes_if_plays) are the solid output: R² 0.787 and mean absolute error 4.0 minutes against what players actually played, on held-out seasons.
  • Expected usage is weak — it barely beats a trailing average (R² 0.342 against 0.331). Lean on the minutes column.
  • PG/SG and SF/PF are a convention, not a label. No source carries the five positions. Guard/forward/centre is learned and holds 97% game to game; the split within guards and within forwards is imposed, holds 87%, and about a quarter of those calls rest on a margin thin enough to be arbitrary.
  • Blank rank gain means the player has almost no chance of playing, so the figure carries no information — every unavailable player ties at zero expected minutes.
  • Team totals do not add up. Each player is predicted independently, so a healthy team lands near its ~236-minute budget while a heavily depleted one falls far short. On a depleted team read the ordering and the rank gains rather than the absolute minutes.
